# Paul Gould

Entrepreneur, product builder, founder/operator, and early-stage investor.

I’m currently co-founder of [HollyHR](https://www.hollyhr.com), where we’re building open, AI-ready HR software for small UK teams. HollyHR brings people records, holiday, sickness, documents, and onboarding into one straightforward system. It’s free for up to 10 active people, with transparent pricing, built-in data export, a documented beta API, signed webhooks, and developer-preview hosted MCP access.

I’m also building [PokerWorks](https://pokerworks.io), a poker platform company working on real-time play-money poker, transparent fairness, and tools for people who build, study, and run poker games.

I founded and continue to run [Trade2Win](https://www.trade2win.com), a long-running UK-centred online community for financial investors and traders.

## Background

I have founded, operated, advised, and invested in internet businesses across software, trading, social gaming, media, and online communities.

Previously, I owned and ran Replay Gaming, the company behind [Replay Poker](https://www.replaypoker.com) which was sold to Legend in 2020. Legend later agreed to be acquired by Genius Sports in a transaction valued at up to $1.2B.

Earlier work includes ForexDesk, a real-time platform for forex traders; NSider2, a large Nintendo community that was later acquired; and Dynamite Partners, a London web studio; Nudge Social Media, an agency that worked with brands including Sony, Mastercard, Britvic, and Sky before being acquired by Syncapse.

I’m especially interested in AI-enabled software, APIs and open platforms, product and customer development, user experience, community-led growth, analytics, social gaming, and financial markets.
